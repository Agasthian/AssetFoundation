Brief Report on 16.08.2026 Meeting

Date: 16th August 2026

×

This meeting was held in two categories.

1.⁠ ⁠For FRESHERS & ACHIEVERS. From 10 am.
2.⁠ ⁠WITH CHIEF & SPECIAL GUESTS. From 11.30 am

The freshers, achievers and mentors assembled between 9.45 am & 10.40 am.

TRUSTEE AZGHAR RAJHA could come only around 11.20 am.

Mr.VARADARAJAN fell ill suddenly previous night and he did not come.

Special Guest Mr.SOUNDARAPANDIAN duly filled in.

Trustee designate KESAVAESWARAN and mentors KATHIRVEL, ARCHANA, DIVYA RAJIV AND MOHANAESWARI participated.

Treasurer RAMACHANDRAN started the meet at 10.30 am. He emphasized punctuality among the beneficiaries. Elaborated the starting / functioning of A.S.S.E.T FOUNDATION.

Mentor MOHANAESWARI cordially welcomed the gathering of 35 students.

Almost every one had the opportunity to speak atleast 5-7 minutes.

Mr.SOUNDARAPANDIAN spoke for 15 minutes. He is a motivator and effectively highlighted need of discipline in our beneficiaries.

There was a revelation. One of our students is சிலம்பம் Champion. Her name is in GUINNESS BOOK OF WORLD RECORDS and other RECORDS too.

THERE WAS A SPECIAL INVITEE MISS.HAMSSIKA. I had invited her.

She studied in M.M.C. four year paramedical course in DIFFERENTLY ABLED. Outstanding student. Finished PG in Mysore. She is a very good Carnatic music singer too.

She spoke eloquently to the gathering, that watched & listened attentively.

PRIZES WERE DISTRIBUTED TO BENEFICIARIES WHO HAVE SCORED ABOVE 85% MARKS.

MISS. SAKTHISWARI / MISS.HARINISRI / MISS.PREETHI USHA LED THE PERFORMANCE.

Prizes for encouragement too were given.

Some beneficiaries came with their parents. Nice of them.

Mrs.DIVYA RAJIV gave vote of thanks.

WE closed the meeting as home prepared (by Aunty RAMAA) vegetarian lunch was ready to be served🙂.

By 2 pm all participants have left.

THIS DOES NOT GIVE YOU THE ACTUAL PROCEEDINGS OF THE MEET.
